Stat Definitions

  • Batting Average - The number of hits divided by the number of at-bats. Must be between 0 and 1.
  • On-Base Percentage - How frequently a batter reaches base per plate appearance. Must be between 0 and 1.
  • Slugging Percentage - The total number of bases per at-bat. Must be between 0 and 4.
  • OPS - On-base plus slugging. This is calculated by adding a player's on-base percentage and slugging percentage together.
